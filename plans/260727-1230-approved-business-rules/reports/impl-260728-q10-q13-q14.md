## What changed

### Q10

- `salaryPeriodsAdminRouter.post('/:period/exclusions')` now rejects missing/unknown `handlingMode` instead of coercing to supplementary.

  - unknown `handlingMode` returns 400;

- Updated `backend/src/tests/q11-salary-post-close.test.ts` isolation fixtures to use distinct supplementary periods so they remain valid under the stricter Q10 rule.

### Q13 / Q14

- Added a transaction-scoped advisory lock in `reviewNoInvoiceDisbursementApproval()` keyed by normalized category/date/payee.
- The lock only activates when the review runs inside the approval transaction, so the approval path serializes same-aggregate decisions without changing read-only/report behavior.
- Added `backend/src/tests/q13-no-invoice-concurrency.test.ts` to prove:
  - same normalized payee/day/category totals across different trips do not under-tier concurrent accountant approvals;
  - losing decisions roll back cleanly and preserve `PENDING`/version state until a director-tier actor approves.
