Prevent duplicate trips when creation fails
QA-030 | P1 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: ADMIN (Quản trị viên); Chrome at 390 × 844 for creation, desktop for readback
Page: https://vantai.tingting.vip/trips/new; readback /trips, /trips/17 and /trips/18
Existing behaviour
Tạo lệnh shows “Leg 1 is invalid (Both origin and destination are required; Distance must be a non-negative number).” and leaves the creation form open, yet TRP-202609-0015 (/trips/17) exists. Correcting the missing endpoints and retrying creates TRP-202609-0016 (/trips/18). Both share QA-FULL-0914-MOBILE; the trip total rises from 14 to 16. The first record opens in Mới tạo state.
Target behaviour
Create one complete trip or none. Rejected submissions must leave no unintended record; retrying the same logical submission must not duplicate it. Validate all generated legs before committing.
Impact
Users can unknowingly create two records for one intended trip and dispatch or reconcile the wrong one.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis: the base trip saves before leg validation or child writes, then retry starts another create. Inspect request sequencing and transaction boundaries; the implementation cause is unverified.
Steps to reproduce
1. As ADMIN at 390 × 844, open /trips/new. Select customer LOG COM, route KCN QUẾ VÕ, BẮC NINH, Xe ngoài and carrier QA CODEX 20260913.
2. Complete required fields with reference QA-FULL-0914-MOBILE, date 14 September 2026, one 20DC and the QA external vehicle details. Keep the two generated 100 km legs, each missing one endpoint.
3. With Sẵn sàng tạo lệnh shown, click Tạo lệnh. Observe the quoted error and the form remaining open.
4. Fill the missing endpoints and submit again. After success, search the reference in /trips: both /trips/17 and /trips/18 appear. Open /trips/17 to verify the persisted first record.
Acceptance criteria
1. Invalid legs leave no trip or dependent records; list/search counts remain unchanged after reload.
2. A corrected submission creates one complete trip, retaining legs, distance, notes and carrier data.
3. Retries of one logical submission must not duplicate it. Allow legitimate trips that reuse a customer reference.
4. Readiness and field errors agree on endpoint/distance requirements. Test partial-write recovery, then reload on mobile, tablet and desktop.
