Keep expense payment status consistent with its supplier ledger

Existing behaviour
Saving QA-EXP-0914-01 as Trả ngay changes /expenses to Đã trả: 123,000 VND paid, zero unpaid. Editor readback retains Trả ngay and says Đây là phiếu đã quyết toán. Fresh supplier reload still owes 223,000 VND: the prior 100,000 plus this 123,000 expense, with three entries and no settlement offset. Only the UI status changed; no actual bank payment occurred.
Target behaviour
Keep payment status linked to the supplier ledger. Show paid/settled only after authorized settlement succeeds. Otherwise, show pending status and explain the required action.
Impact
Conflicting payment and debt views risk duplicate payment or missed reconciliation.
Cause and investigation
Inspect status transitions and ledger posting; no backend cause is established. Distinct from QA-086’s initial approval failure.
Steps to reproduce
1. Use the 123,000 VND Ghi nợ expense for CÔNG TY CP SBI, receipt QA-EXP-0914-01, or an equivalent fixture.
2. Edit Trạng thái thanh toán to Trả ngay, enter the required staging reason and click Cập nhật.
3. Verify the paid expense list/editor, then reload /payables/4 and compare the remaining charge and supplier balance.
Acceptance criteria
1. After authorized settlement and reload, expense status, paid/unpaid totals and supplier debt agree.
2. Failed or pending settlement never reports paid/settled; show clear recovery.
3. Retries post once; supported reversals restore consistent status and ledger history.
4. Preserve unrelated debt, permissions and audit links; verify desktop, tablet and phone readback.
Requirement reference
Observed expense status change and fresh supplier-ledger readback.
