Existing behaviour
For Bill EHPH26080202, the customer-service detail ledger shows HÀ AN, plate 51K-PROBE and Đã phân xe. The dispatcher detail plan shows Chưa phân nhà xe, CUS sẽ bổ sung and Đã phát lệnh cho tài xế for the same unnumbered container. Assignment and issue status are contradictory across roles.
Target behaviour
Both workspaces must derive current carrier, vehicle and dispatch status consistently. If a historical or canceled trip exists, distinguish it from the active plan and do not present its resources as the current assignment.
Impact
CUS and dispatch may coordinate against different vehicles or assume a driver has been issued work that is not currently allocated.
Cause and investigation
Likely inconsistent precedence between planned allocation and historical trip resources. A prior source note describes cleanup of a 51K-PROBE trip, but its current backend status was not independently checked; do not treat that note as proof of the root cause.
Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as thanhdc, open Chi tiết lô hàng, choose Tất cả and search EHPH26080202.
2. Record carrier HÀ AN and vehicle 51K-PROBE in Phân xe.
3. Sign in as dungnv, open Kế hoạch chi tiết, search EHPH26080202 and compare allocation and issue status.
Acceptance criteria
1. Both roles show the same current carrier, plate and meaningful issue state for EHPH26080202.
2. Canceled or superseded assignments do not leak into the active assignment display.
3. Verify assigned, unassigned, canceled-trip and reassigned examples after page reload.
