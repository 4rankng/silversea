Describe negative earnings balances without assuming excess advances
QA-079 | P3 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: DRIVER · Dương Văn Thực (dvthuc) · phone 390 × 844
Page: /my-earnings
Existing behaviour
For September 2026, the headline says Đã tạm ứng vượt above −60,000 VND. The same screen shows Đã tạm ứng/đã thanh toán as 0 and Khấu trừ kỷ luật as 60,000 VND, with base and trip earnings at zero. The displayed negative balance comes from the deduction, yet its headline attributes it to excess advances.
Target behaviour
Use a neutral signed-balance label, such as Số dư sổ lương, or explain the actual contributing components. Show an excess-advance label only when supported by actual advances and the applicable balance rule.
Impact
The driver may believe an advance was paid or exceeded an allowance when the screen records no such payment.
Cause and investigation
The headline may be selected only from the balance sign. Inspect label selection and available components; implementation is unverified. Deduction eligibility is tracked separately in QA-077; this task does not dispute the displayed arithmetic.
Steps to reproduce
1. Open /my-earnings for Dương Văn Thực and select September 2026.
2. Compare the −60,000 VND headline with zero advances/payments and the 60,000 VND discipline deduction.
Acceptance criteria
1. A negative balance caused solely by deductions must not claim that excess advances were paid.
2. Cover deduction-only, actual advance, mixed, zero and positive balances with accurate Vietnamese wording.
3. Preserve the balance calculation and component amounts; keep the label consistent on phone, tablet and desktop.
Requirement reference
Observed driver earnings summary and its displayed balance components.
Evidence 1
