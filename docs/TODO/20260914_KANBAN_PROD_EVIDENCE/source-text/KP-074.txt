Keep expense and supplier debt pending until the promised review is complete
QA-086 | P1 | Bug | QA PASSED (API/DB rung; responsive sliver → UI pile)

Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: ADMIN · desktop 1440 × 900; tablet ledger 834 × 1112
Page: /expenses/new → /expenses/1/edit → /payables/4
Existing behaviour
The form states: “Khoản chi và công nợ chỉ được ghi nhận sau khi một người kiểm tra và một người khác phê duyệt.” One Admin click on Gửi duyệt chi phí nevertheless records a 123,000 VND Ghi nợ expense. SBI’s ledger gains that expense, raising its balance from 100,000 to 223,000 VND. No checker or approver was invoked before this readback.
Target behaviour
Submission must create a pending request. Post the expense and supplier debt only after a checker and a different approver complete review, with clear stages and actors.
Impact
A single submission creates an economic obligation before the promised review. No cash transfer occurred.
Cause and investigation
Submission behavior conflicts with the stated approval contract. Inspect routing and posting conditions; backend implementation and global permission bypass are unverified.
Steps to reproduce
1. As Admin, enter supplier CÔNG TY CP SBI, category QA AUDIT 0914 Recurring expense, Ghi nợ, 123000 VND, effective September 14–30 and receipt QA-EXP-0914-01.
2. Enter the required reason and click Gửi duyệt chi phí once, without review or approval.
3. Read /expenses and /payables/4: both already include the new 123,000 VND obligation.
Acceptance criteria
1. Submission leaves posted expenses and supplier debt unchanged while displaying a pending request.
2. Require a checker and a different approver; show pending, rejected and approved states accurately.
3. Approval posts exactly once; retries, rejection and repeated clicks cannot create premature or duplicate debt.
4. Verify approval, posting, readback and role boundaries across desktop, tablet and phone; retain dates and receipt.
Requirement reference
Observed form promise compared with the resulting expense and supplier ledger.
