Keep automatic tire lifecycle dates consistent with the business date
QA-050 | P2 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: Admin; Chrome desktop 1440 × 900 and phone 390 × 844
Page: https://vantai.tingting.vip/fleet/trailers/40/tires
Existing behaviour
QA-TIRE-0914-01 initially showed Ngày lắp 2026-09-14 and 0 ngày after creation and transfer. Removing it to spare stock and reinstalling it immediately changed these to 2026-09-13 and 1 ngày; both persisted after reload. Disposal during the same session also displayed Ngày thanh lý 2026-09-13. No lifecycle date was entered manually, and both Vietnam and Singapore were already on 14 September.
Target behaviour
Generate creation, reinstallation and disposal dates consistently in the agreed business timezone. Preserve date-only values through saving and display; calculate elapsed running days using the same date basis.
Impact
Fresh lifecycle events appear a day old, distorting tire service age and maintenance history.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis: lifecycle actions use inconsistent UTC/local date conversion. The responsible frontend, API or storage implementation has not been established.
Steps to reproduce
1. Create a test tire on 15H-021.39, then transfer it to QA-0914-RM. Observe today's installation date and 0 ngày.
2. Choose Tháo lốp → default Giữ làm dự phòng. Choose Lắp lốp lên xe, select the position only and confirm. Reload and compare dates.
3. Dispose of the test tire with reason QA FULL AUDIT 20260914 disposal test. Inspect Ngày thanh lý.
Acceptance criteria
1. Creation and same-day reinstallation display today's business date and 0 elapsed days; same-day disposal displays that date.
2. Values remain consistent after reload and across desktop, tablet and phone.
3. Cover UTC/business-date disagreement around midnight, the local day boundary and date-only round trips. Verify historical dates remain unchanged.
Requirement reference
Staging tire lifecycle review, 14 September 2026; trailer QA-0914-RM.
