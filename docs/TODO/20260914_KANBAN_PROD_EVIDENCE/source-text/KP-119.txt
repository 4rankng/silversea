Keep the customer discrepancy form within the visible row
QA-108 | P2 | Bug | DONE
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: CUSTOMER · qa.audit.customer.0914 · 1440 × 900; comparison at 834 × 1112 and 390 × 844
Page: /portal/shipments · Cần xác nhận
Existing behaviour
Opening Báo sai lệch in the desktop shipment table expands the editor inside a narrow action cell. The 220 px reason field extends about 62 px beyond the visible table edge, and Hủy is partly clipped. A horizontal scrollbar is present below the table, so revealing the right edge requires moving away from the active form to scroll. Phone and tablet captures show the complete field and actions.
Target behaviour
Keep the expanded discrepancy editor and its actions visible together. Use a full-width expanded row, a suitably sized popover or a sheet with shipment context. Fit the form to the available width with modest padding, a useful reason field and clear Submit and Cancel actions.
Impact
Customers must reposition the desktop table while composing a discrepancy and cannot see the complete field and Cancel action in the initial editing view.
Cause and investigation
The observed editor exceeds its action cell. Review the expanded layout and overflow containment. No submission failure, data loss or permanently inaccessible control is claimed.
Steps to reproduce
1. As the Customer account, open /portal/shipments at 1440 × 900 and select Cần xác nhận.
2. For SHP-2609-00002, container EMCU6163403, open Báo sai lệch.
3. Focus Lý do sai lệch. Observe that the field and Hủy extend beyond the table’s right edge; the horizontal scrollbar is below the records.
Acceptance criteria
1. Opening the editor keeps the entire reason field and both actions inside the visible content area without table-level horizontal scrolling.
2. Preserve the shipment identity and existing permissions, validation, submission and cancellation behavior; do not trigger the row’s navigation while editing.
3. At 390, 834 and 1440 px, keep labels and actions readable, spacing compact and touch targets usable. Avoid nested decorative cards or unnecessary blank space.
4. Verify keyboard entry, focus order, visible focus, long reasons, inline errors, pending submission and cancellation; opening or resizing the editor must preserve the draft.
Requirement reference
Live Customer discrepancy editor and the requested compact responsive interface.

Resolution
Moved the dispute form out of the narrow 12 %-width action cell into a full-width expanded row beneath the triggering table row. The “Báo sai lệch” button remains in the action cell; clicking it now reveals a colSpan’d <tr> with the textarea and Confirm / Cancel buttons rendered side by side. The row pair is wrapped in a <Fragment key> so React’s list reconciliation stays correct.
