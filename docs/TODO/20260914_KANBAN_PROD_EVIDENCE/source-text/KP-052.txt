Provide factory invoice information in driver trip details

Existing behaviour
Trip 12, container EMCU6163403, identifies ASKEY-2 as its factory. Thông tin xuất hóa đơn shows CÔNG TY TNHH MỘT THÀNH VIÊN LONG MINH, Khu 2, Phường Võ Cường, Tỉnh Bắc Ninh, and MST 2300540419. These customer details do not provide the factory invoice information requested in the wireframe.
Target behaviour
Show a clearly identified factory invoice profile with its configured legal name, invoice address and MST. Preserve valid customer billing as a distinct party when needed. Use the configured invoice address; do not assume it is the delivery address. Identify missing configuration instead of substituting another party's values.
Impact
Drivers cannot obtain the requested factory invoice details from this screen and must confirm the party and information elsewhere.
Cause and investigation
The displayed values match the Long Minh customer profile. Investigate invoice party selection and factory profile mapping. Invoice issuance and the factory's tax ID were not verified.
Steps to reproduce
1. As dvthuc, open the accepted EMCU6163403 trip at /my-trips/12.
2. Expand Thông tin lệnh and confirm ASKEY-2. Expand Thông tin xuất hóa đơn and observe the Long Minh company, address and MST.
3. Compare with IMG_6370 and IMG_6371. If this fixture changes, use a trip where customer and factory differ.
Acceptance criteria
1. For different customer and factory entities, show the configured factory legal name, invoice address and MST with an explicit party label.
2. Customer billing remains correctly attributed. Factory details neither overwrite customer billing nor silently fall back to it.
3. Identify missing factory fields. Verify against configured values; do not invent an ASKEY tax ID.
4. Keep the section compact and readable at 390px. Reload preserves the correct party and values.
Requirement reference
IMG_6366.png, IMG_6370.png and IMG_6371.png require full factory name, address and MST in driver invoice information. Related QA-006 covers ordinary order identity and address labeling.
