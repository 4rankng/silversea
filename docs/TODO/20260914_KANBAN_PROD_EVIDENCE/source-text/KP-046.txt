Open driver attachments in a readable full image viewer
QA-018 | P2 | Enhancement | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 13 September 2026
Role: Lái xe — Bùi Tiến Dũng; login btdung; Chrome mobile viewport 390 × 844
Page: https://vantai.tingting.vip/my-trips/29 — SỐ CONT & SEAL, Hình ảnh
Existing behaviour
Saved container, seal and report images loaded after upload and reload, but Hình ảnh showed small cropped thumbnails. Clicking each opened no enlarged view, dialog or tab. Image text was cropped or too small to inspect comfortably. In contrast, the separate ePOD workflow opened a viewer with zoom, reset, Escape/close and gallery navigation.
Target behaviour
Connect compact attachment thumbnails to the existing ePOD viewing pattern. Initially fit the whole image, support zoom/pan, and close back to the same attachment and scroll position.
Impact
Drivers cannot comfortably verify container/seal details or read reports from cropped thumbnails, although saved images are available.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis: thumbnails lack an implemented or discoverable viewer action. Inspect the interaction and image source; no component-level cause is established.
Steps to reproduce
1. As btdung at 390 × 844, use an active trip with all three test-image types saved. The observed trip 29 was later completed.
2. Reload and confirm all three Hình ảnh thumbnails load.
3. Click each image and attempt full-image inspection. No enlarged view or new tab opened in the observed run.
Acceptance criteria
1. Each populated image opens through a named touch, pointer and keyboard action; empty slots do not imply an available image.
2. Initially fit the full image; support zoom and pan to inspect source details.
3. Visible close and Escape return focus to the opener and preserve scroll position.
4. All three types preview after reload; opening/closing never changes or deletes attachments.
5. Keep compact thumbnails and readable labels; avoid oversized inline image panels.
Requirement reference
Exploratory staging review, 13 September 2026; user direction for compact, smooth data-heavy UI.
Related: 20260911_2.docx FEATURE 2 and 20260912_6.docx. Full-size viewing is an enhancement proposal.
