Existing behaviour
The revenue chart says “Chưa có chuyến nào hoàn thành trong tháng 9/2026” and “Hoàn thành chuyến để xem xu hướng doanh thu”. The same report shows “4 chuyến × giá cước chặng”; vehicle analysis lists one trip for 15H-104.03 and three for Xe ngoài. A known completed trip, TRP-202609-0013, was matched through the vehicle drilldown. Monetary values are zero. The message incorrectly describes the trip state; no arithmetic defect is asserted.
Target behaviour
Base the no-trip message on completed-trip count. For completed trips with zero amounts, use accurate zero-value messaging. If monetary inputs are demonstrably missing, identify the missing data and the relevant next step.
Impact
The message directs managers to complete trips instead of explaining zero or missing revenue.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis: the chart treats an all-zero financial series as no completed trips. Inspect the empty-state condition and its data source; the reason for the zero monetary values is outside this finding.
Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as qa.audit.manager.0914, open /finance and select September 2026.
2. Read the revenue-chart empty message, then compare Doanh thu vận tải on the same page: 4 chuyến × giá cước chặng.
3. Open Phân tích lãi gộp theo phương tiện. Compare 15H-104.03 with one trip and Xe ngoài with three; use the vehicle drilldown to inspect the underlying records.
Acceptance criteria
1. A month with no completed trips shows a genuine no-trip state; a month with completed trips and zero amounts does not claim that none are completed.
2. Distinguish valid zero amounts from missing monetary inputs. Display a zero-value chart or an accurate explanation, with action text appropriate to the known condition.
3. Chart messaging, trip counts, vehicle drilldowns and selected period agree after reload and when switching Ngày/Tháng. Verify mobile, tablet and desktop.
4. Keep financial calculations unchanged while correcting the empty-state selection.
