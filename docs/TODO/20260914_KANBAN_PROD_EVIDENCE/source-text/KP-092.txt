Reconcile the Ops wallet with approved advance returns
QA-054 | P2 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: OPS hoangnh (Nguyễn Huy Hoàng); desktop 1440 × 900
Page: /my-settlements/1 and /ops/wallet
Existing behaviour
Approved settlement PT-2609-0001 records 75,000 VND received, zero expenses, 75,000 VND returned and zero difference. For the same employee, the wallet still shows SỐ DƯ HIỆN TẠI 51,544 VND, Đã ứng 75,000 VND and Chờ duyệt 23,456 VND. Its displayed balance equals 75,000 − 23,456, with no visible deduction for the approved full return.
Target behaviour
Reconcile available funds with approved returns exactly once. Clearly separate cumulative advances, returns and pending reservations so returned funds are not presented as available.
Impact
Ops staff can overestimate available advance funds and request or spend against a balance that conflicts with the approved settlement.
Cause and investigation
The wallet may omit settled returns from its balance, or its labels may represent incompatible totals. Confirm calculation and status rules; the implementation was not inspected.
Steps to reproduce
1. As hoangnh, open /my-settlements/1. Confirm PT-2609-0001 is Đã duyệt with the full 75,000 VND return and zero difference.
2. Open /ops/wallet for September 2026. Compare its 51,544 VND current balance and 23,456 VND pending expense with the approved full return.
Acceptance criteria
1. Approved full and partial returns reconcile with the wallet after navigation and reload, without applying a return twice.
2. Label available cash, cumulative advances, returns and pending reservations distinctly; do not show returned funds as spendable.
3. Define how pending expenses affect availability. Test approval and rejection against that policy; the required negative-balance presentation is not assumed.
4. Retest a new advance after settlement and verify consistent employee, period and settlement status across both pages.
Requirement reference
Chrome staging review, 14 September 2026; approved advance return and Ops wallet readback.
