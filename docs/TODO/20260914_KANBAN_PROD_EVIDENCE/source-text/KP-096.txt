Existing behaviour
The form offers QA Audit 0914 - Staging (QA0914), but saving rejects it with “Invalid enum value” and “received 'QA0914' (expenseType)”, listing fixed internal codes. The completed draft stays open; Chi phí phát sinh remains 0 and no expense is created.
Target behaviour
Save selectable configured categories with their correct identity and rules. Explain unusable categories before submission with actionable Vietnamese feedback; retain the draft on errors.
Impact
Ops cannot record the expense using the only offered category, blocking cost completion and its downstream review.
Cause and investigation
Category selection and expenseType validation appear inconsistent. Inspect their mapping and shared rules; the implementation and failing layer are unverified.
