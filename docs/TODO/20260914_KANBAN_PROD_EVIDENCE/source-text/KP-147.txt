Remove approval steps from financial configuration
QA-110 | P2 | Enhancement | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: ADMIN · 1440 × 900, 834 × 1112 and 390 × 844
Page: /config/app-settings · Chính sách báo cáo and Hồ sơ tài chính xe
Existing behaviour
Both financial tabs still offer Gửi yêu cầu phê duyệt. Reporting policy labels history Lịch sử đã duyệt and refers to an approved version when the effective month exists. The vehicle form uses the same approval action, contrary to the requirement to remove internal approvals.
Target behaviour
Let authorized users save valid policy and vehicle financial versions directly. Apply each on its effective date with clear saved state, version history and audit trail. Remove approval requests, reviewer and approver steps, approval-only labels and blockers.
Impact
Administrators are directed into an obsolete approval process instead of completing an authorized configuration update.
Cause and investigation
Align both tabs and submission paths with the simplified workflow. Renaming the button or automatically marking requests approved is insufficient. Submission internals were not inspected.
Steps to reproduce
1. As Admin, open /config/app-settings and select Chính sách báo cáo; inspect the version form, history and Gửi yêu cầu phê duyệt action.
2. Switch to Hồ sơ tài chính xe and choose 15C-167.31. The monthly vehicle form also presents Gửi yêu cầu phê duyệt.
Acceptance criteria
1. A valid authorized save completes directly, persists after reload and takes effect on the stated date without a second user or an approval queue.
2. Keep input validation, permitted date ranges, closed-period rules, role access and clear conflict recovery. Removing approval must not bypass ordinary data checks.
3. Retain meaningful version and change history with actor and time. Resolve pre-existing pending requests during migration without duplicate versions or silent data loss.
4. Remove approval-only wording, statuses and request actions in both tabs. Show clear success, validation and save-error feedback at 390, 834 and 1440 px with usable keyboard and touch controls.
Requirement reference
User requirement to remove internal approval flows; current financial configuration UI.
