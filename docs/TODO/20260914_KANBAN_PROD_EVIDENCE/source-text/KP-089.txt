Existing behaviour
Thêm rơ-moóc instructs users to assign a trailer by editing the tractor and choosing it from a list. The complete Sửa xe editor for 15H-021.39 has no trailer selector. It contains vehicle details, carrier, fuel, maintenance and notes only. QA-0914-RM was successfully created as 40FT and edited to 20FT, but the displayed assignment path cannot be completed.
Target behaviour
Provide an authorized, searchable trailer selector in the indicated truck editor, showing the current assignment and available trailers. Keep the guidance aligned with the working assignment action.
Impact
An administrator can create a trailer but cannot attach it to a tractor through the workflow the app instructs them to use.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis: assignment guidance and the current truck form are out of sync. No source-code, permission or datastore cause has been established.
Steps to reproduce
1. As Admin, open /fleet → Thêm rơ-moóc. Read the instruction below Ghi chú; create a clearly identified trailer.
2. Follow the instruction: open truck 15H-021.39 (observed row 19) → Sửa. Inspect the entire editor; no trailer selection is available.
Acceptance criteria
1. Search trailers by plate, distinguish their types and current assignments, and select an eligible trailer from the truck editor.
2. Handle an already assigned trailer explicitly under the existing assignment rules; prevent silent conflicting assignments.
3. Save and read back the relationship in both truck and trailer views; reopen and reload to verify persistence. Show actionable save errors.
4. Keep guidance accurate and the selector compact, keyboard accessible and usable on phone, tablet and desktop.
