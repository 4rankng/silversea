Keep factory identity prominent and label its address in driver details
QA-006 | P2 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 13 September 2026
Role: Lái xe — Bùi Quang Hường; login bqhuong; Chrome mobile viewport 390 × 844
Page: https://vantai.tingting.vip/my-trips/20
Existing behaviour
On initial entry with the task summary expanded, its heading shows the route “KCN QUẾ VÕ, BẮC NINH”. The same summary prioritizes “NEWEB-1” after being collapsed, so the main identity changes with presentation state. Expanded Thông tin lệnh displays the factory street address “Lô đất CN01, Khu công nghiệp Đồng Văn III, Phường Đồng Văn, Tỉnh Ninh Bình, Việt Nam” under TUYẾN rather than the requested ĐỊA CHỈ NHÀ MÁY label. This task does not claim that the stored full factory name is missing or incorrectly truncated.
Target behaviour
Make the factory abbreviation the primary identity in the initial task summary, with the route subordinate and still available. In expanded order information, show the full factory name when available and label its street address ĐỊA CHỈ NHÀ MÁY. Keep the abbreviation in compact/collapsed identification and apply an explicit fallback when no separate full name exists.
Impact
Drivers must infer whether TUYẾN represents a transport route or the factory street address. Changing the main heading between route and factory when the summary is collapsed also makes destination identification less consistent.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis only: the expanded and collapsed summary states may select different primary fields, and the address label may remain from the previous route-oriented presentation. Verify the rendered state mappings and available factory fields before changing them; no code-level cause is asserted.
Steps to reproduce
1. On 13 September 2026, sign in to staging as bqhuong in Chrome at 390 × 844.
2. From Đã nhận, open TSTU4182600 with Xem chi tiết to reach /my-trips/20.
3. On initial entry, read the route-led task heading; collapse the summary with its chevron and observe the factory abbreviation NEWEB-1 becoming the main heading.
4. Expand Thông tin lệnh if necessary and inspect the TUYẾN label above the factory street address.
Acceptance criteria
1. The initial task summary identifies the factory first and retains the route in a subordinate position; changing the summary's expanded state does not remove the factory identity.
2. The factory street-address field is labeled ĐỊA CHỈ NHÀ MÁY, while any separate transport route remains clearly distinguished.
3. With a fixture whose full name differs from its abbreviation, expanded order information displays the full factory name and the compact summary retains the abbreviation.
4. A fixture without a separate full name has a clear fallback and does not display undefined, empty duplicate fields or fabricated names.
5. Independent section collapse, container details, ports, phone/contact information and invoicing information remain usable at 390px width.
