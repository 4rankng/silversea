Existing behaviour
Appointment-time lines, cargo weights, customer notes and the CUS sẽ bổ sung hint use very pale gray text on a white table background. In the TESTSS2609 filtered view these operational values are much harder to read than adjacent bold container IDs. The issue was confirmed visually; an exact contrast ratio was not measured.
Target behaviour
Keep secondary data readable at normal zoom while retaining clear hierarchy. Use a text color meeting at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal-size text against every table background and avoid treating operational values as disabled UI.
Impact
Frequent scanning becomes harder, especially on dim displays or for users with reduced vision.
Cause and investigation
Likely an overly light shared muted-text token or inherited opacity. Confirm computed colors and opacity before changing styles.
Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as dungnv and open Kế hoạch chi tiết.
2. Search TESTSS2609 and read the time, weight and customer-note text at normal zoom.
3. Compare readability at 1280 pixels and in narrow cards.
Acceptance criteria
1. Normal-size operational text meets 4.5:1 contrast on white, highlighted and alternate row backgrounds.
2. Notes, times and weights are comfortably readable without increasing browser zoom.
3. Disabled actions remain distinguishable without dimming unrelated shipment data.
