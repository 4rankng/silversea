Keep pending discipline out of driver pay
QA-077 | P2 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: ADMIN desktop 1440 × 900; DRIVER dvthuc phone 390 × 844
Page: /penalties; driver /my-penalties and /my-earnings
Existing behaviour
Admin created a 60,000 VND discipline record for Dương Văn Thực, linked to TRP-202609-0017. A fresh Admin login/reload still shows it under Chờ duyệt; no approval was performed. The driver already sees a 60,000 discipline deduction and current earnings of -60,000, with advances/payments at 0. The driver’s discipline view does not explain the pending status.
Target behaviour
Exclude pending records from applied payroll deductions and show their status consistently. If Admin creation is intentionally an immediate approval, record and display that approved state under the agreed policy, rather than leaving the item pending.
Impact
The mismatch undermines payroll trust. Admin could also confirm the driver’s -60,000 payroll snapshot while the global period stayed open. This was payroll confirmation, not discipline approval; no external payment was made.
Cause and investigation
Hypothesis: payroll inclusion and discipline status use different lifecycle rules. Trace creation, approval and payroll projection; no implementation cause is confirmed.
Steps to reproduce
1. As Admin, create the 60,000 VND record for Dương Văn Thực and TRP-202609-0017; do not approve it.
2. Select Chờ duyệt, then repeat after a fresh Admin login/reload; confirm the matching record remains pending.
3. Sign in as dvthuc for September 2026; compare /my-penalties and /my-earnings, including Khấu trừ kỷ luật.
Acceptance criteria
1. Pending records do not change applied deductions or current payable earnings; both roles can see the pending status.
2. Authorized approval applies the deduction exactly once and updates both views. Rejection/cancellation follows the defined policy without leaving a pending deduction applied.
3. Verify create, reload, approval and cancellation across Admin and Driver on desktop, tablet and phone; distinguish approved deductions from pending amounts.
Requirement reference
Discipline help promises payroll deductions after approval; cross-role staging check, 14 September 2026.
