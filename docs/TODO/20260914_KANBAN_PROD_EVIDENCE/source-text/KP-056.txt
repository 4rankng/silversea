Existing behaviour
The new-order banner says Nhận lệnh ngay, không cần chờ Ops and App tạm bỏ qua xác nhận hiện trường. This prominent text exposes temporary application behavior and internal terminology above an existing Nhận lệnh vận chuyển action, consuming space without plainly stating the driver’s immediate step.
Target behaviour
Use a short, stable task instruction, such as Kiểm tra thông tin chuyến rồi chọn Nhận lệnh. Accurately state active prerequisites or consequences. Remove internal implementation and temporary-rollout wording; keep guidance compact and visually secondary.
Impact
Internal terminology makes routine acceptance feel provisional and distracts from checking trip information on a small screen.
Cause and investigation
Design hypothesis: rollout wording is being used as normal driver guidance. Confirm active business rules before changing it; preserve any required operational step.
Steps to reproduce
1. At 390 × 844, open an owned newly issued trip as its assigned driver before acceptance. The screenshot shows QATU0900043 as bqhuong; that fixture was later completed.
2. Read the banner above Thông tin lệnh and compare it with the Nhận lệnh vận chuyển action.
Acceptance criteria
1. Use plain Vietnamese without unexplained Ops terminology or App tạm bỏ qua implementation language.
2. Copy accurately describes acceptance prerequisites and effects. No required confirmation is removed or implied away.
3. At 390px width, guidance stays compact and subordinate to a clear acceptance action, preserving text readability and usable touch targets.
Requirement reference
User UI/UX direction, 13 September 2026: professional, compact and user-friendly presentation.
