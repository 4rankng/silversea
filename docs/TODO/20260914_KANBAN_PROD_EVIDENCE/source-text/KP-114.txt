Exclude cancelled penalties from active rankings and discipline totals
QA-083 | P2 | Bug | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: ADMIN · desktop 1440 × 900
Page: /penalties
Existing behaviour
After cancelling Dương Văn Thực's 60,000 VND record and reloading, Đã hủy shows 1, Chờ duyệt shows 0 and the ledger row is struck through. His ranking still shows position 39, zero safe days, one violation, 60,000 VND and grade A. Before this record, he had 10 safe days, zero violations/fines and A+. Monthly totals still show one violation, 60,000 VND and 38/39 compliant drivers. Payroll correctly reverses the deduction to zero.
Target behaviour
Exclude cancelled records from active violation/fine totals, safety streaks and ranking calculations. Recompute affected summaries consistently while retaining the cancelled record and its audit history.
Impact
A voided record continues to lower the driver's standing and misstate the fleet's active discipline totals.
Cause and investigation
Ranking and aggregate calculations may use different cancellation eligibility from the ledger and payroll. Verify the calculation inputs and refresh behavior; no source or API cause is confirmed. Pending-record payroll eligibility is separate QA-077.
Steps to reproduce
1. In /penalties, use a staging record equivalent to QA AUDIT 0914 - staging only: Dương Văn Thực, TRP-202609-0017, 60,000 VND.
2. Cancel the record with a reason, reload and select Đã hủy.
3. Compare the cancelled row with the driver's ranking and monthly summary; the cancelled penalty still contributes.
Acceptance criteria
1. Cancellation removes the record's contribution from active counts, fine totals, compliance grade and safety streak immediately and after reload.
2. Recompute from remaining eligible records across each ranking period; do not reset unrelated records or drivers.
3. Retain the cancelled record and cancellation audit details in history, clearly distinct from active totals.
4. Verify desktop, tablet and phone summaries; preserve the correctly working payroll reversal.
Requirement reference
Observed cancellation, ledger readback and ranking comparison for the only September 2026 discipline record.
Evidence 1
