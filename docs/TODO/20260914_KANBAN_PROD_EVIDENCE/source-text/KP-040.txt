Compact dispatcher phone cards and keep container codes readable
QA-011 | P2 | Enhancement | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 13 September 2026
Role: Dispatcher dungnv; Chrome viewport 390 by 844, with 820-pixel comparison; 13 September 2026
Page: https://vantai.tingting.vip/dispatch-detail
Existing behaviour
At 390 pixels wide, detailed-plan cards retain large empty areas between information groups. Short container-type codes such as 20'DC wrap the final C onto a separate line. The information remains accessible, but these gaps and fragmented codes make scanning slower and consume scarce phone space. The stable 820-pixel card layout is more compact. This finding does not concern the mobile filter drawer, which fitted correctly on retest.
Target behaviour
Use a compact phone layout with related fields grouped together and no empty grid cells reserving unnecessary height. Keep short container and equipment codes together on one line while allowing natural wrapping for names, addresses and notes.
Impact
Dispatchers need extra scrolling and visual effort to compare containers on a phone, despite relatively little information per card.
Cause and investigation
Likely inherited desktop column allocation or fixed grid spans in the narrow card variant. This is a visual hypothesis; inspect the layout rules before choosing a change.
Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as dungnv and open Kế hoạch chi tiết at a 390 by 844 Chrome viewport.
2. Close navigation and filters, then inspect the unfiltered container cards.
3. Observe the unused internal areas and a 20'DC type split across lines; compare with the same page at 820 pixels.
Acceptance criteria
1. At 360, 390 and 430 pixels, short type codes stay intact and remain readable at normal zoom.
2. Related identity, schedule, allocation and note fields form a compact readable sequence without large empty grid areas.
3. Long real names and multiline notes wrap without clipping, and all edit and completion controls remain accessible.
4. The desktop table and stable tablet card layout retain their existing data and actions.
