Make penalty records reachable before the expanded driver ranking
QA-076 | P2 | Enhancement | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: ADMIN · phone 390 × 844; tablet 834 × 1112
Page: /penalties
Existing behaviour
All 39 driver rankings precede Sổ biên bản vi phạm, its search and Chờ duyệt filter. Phone rankings are large expanded cards: the 788 px content viewport has 8,480 px scroll height, and the ledger was reached at about 7,559 px scroll. Tablet also expands all 39 entries before the ledger. Lập biên bản is already available at the top.
Target behaviour
Make the record ledger and pending-record review immediately reachable through primary placement or a clear tab/jump action. Keep rankings available in a compact, collapsible or paginated section, preserving the current creation action.
Impact
Routine record lookup requires traversing a long ranking section, especially on smaller screens.
Cause and investigation
The page places the complete ranking before record review. This is a layout priority issue; no ranking-calculation or approval-permission failure is claimed.
Steps to reproduce
1. Open /penalties as Admin for September 2026 with 39 ranked drivers and one recorded case.
2. On phone, locate record search and Chờ duyệt; scroll past every ranking card to reach them.
3. Repeat on tablet and compare the effort needed to reach the same ledger.
Acceptance criteria
1. From the initial viewport, reach record search and pending records directly without traversing 39 entries.
2. Retain complete rankings, their time filters and counts through compact expansion or pagination.
3. Preserve Lập biên bản at the top and maintain ledger filters, record actions and position after review.
4. Verify phone, tablet and desktop; tab/jump controls must support keyboard use and move focus clearly.
Requirement reference
Observed /penalties layout and measured phone scrolling; proposed improvement for efficient review of dense operational data.
