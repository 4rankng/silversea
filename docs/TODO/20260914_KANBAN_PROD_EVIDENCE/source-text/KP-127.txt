Make customer shipment cards easier to scan on phones
QA-044 | P3 | Enhancement | TODO
Environment: Staging https://vantai.tingting.vip
Reviewed: 14 September 2026
Role: Customer qa.audit.customer.0914, scoped to Long Minh; Chrome phone 390 × 844
Page: https://vantai.tingting.vip/portal/shipments
Existing behaviour
A phone shipment card occupies about 276 pixels, including a blank Mốc nghiệp vụ row, Trở ngại: Không có and repeated tracking/status copy. At 390 × 844, one complete card and part of the next fit below the controls, slowing comparison across six shipments.
Target behaviour
Prioritize shipment identity, container, clearly labeled status and the record action. Omit empty milestone rows, show actual blockers and group secondary metadata. Preserve full detail access without enlarging components or text.
Impact
Customers must scroll through repeated and empty fields to compare shipments and reach the next record.
Cause and investigation
Design hypothesis: table columns become separate mobile rows even when blank or non-actionable. Review responsive card composition; no missing data or failed action is established.
Steps to reproduce
1. As Customer, open Lô hàng của tôi at 390 × 844 and select Đang vận chuyển 6.
2. Inspect SHP-2609-00002: note the blank milestone row, no-blocker row and separate repeated tracking/status lines.
3. Scroll through subsequent cards and compare identifying data with empty or repeated metadata.
Acceptance criteria
1. Empty milestone fields do not create dedicated rows. Actual milestones and blockers remain clearly visible when present.
2. Group secondary metadata and remove redundant copy while retaining shipment/container identity, meaningful status and Xem hồ sơ.
3. At 390 × 844 with these fixtures, aim to show two complete compact cards in the current content area, with readable text and usable touch actions.
4. Verify long identifiers, multiple containers and populated blockers; retain the compact desktop table. Status wording is handled separately in QA-042.
Requirement reference
Exploratory staging review, 14 September 2026.
User direction: professional, compact, data-heavy UI without oversized components or text.
