# Testplan — card 161026204709: rev-merge 100%/0₫ lane hidden in the phone 'Chi tiết' collapse

F-2 (card 161026113900, read-helper `revenue-override-read.service.ts`,
commit b859d0b8) makes both money boards read the override channel — the
service-level law is green (`lcl-grouping.test.ts` F2-01..F2-05). This card is
the FE display gap left open ("rung-3 UI drive still open"): the boards' phone
row renderers swallow the lane into the collapsed `details > summary "Chi tiết"`
block of the shared `LedgerRecordList`, so the spec §3.2/§4 row
(Lô 1 = 100% lane price, member = 0₫ + "Ghép cước cùng Bill X" reason) is
never visibly rendered at ≤640px.

## Defect

`LedgerRecordList` renders `primary` facts on the card face and folds every
non-primary fact into a closed `<details><summary>Chi tiết</summary>…`. Two
callers leave the cước-thu money fact non-primary:

- CUS "Chi phí - Quyết toán" phone facts
  (`features/shipments/debit/ShipmentDebitRecords.tsx`): the `freight` fact
  ("Cước vận tải (Auto)", value = `freightFact(row)` = money + the
  `freightOverrideReason` note) carries no `primary` flag.
- Kế toán "Kế hoạch điều động tổng hợp" phone facts
  (`pages/accounting/AccountingDebitRecords.tsx`):
  `PRIMARY_FACTS = {ngay, container, tongThu, tong1, loiNhuan, doiSoat}` omits
  `cuocThu` ("Cước thu (tự động)").

Desktop renders both visibly (CUS table `ShipmentDebitPage.tsx` money cell +
note; Kế toán matrix `AccountingDebitClosePage.columns.tsx` `cuocThu` cell,
member reason "Ghép cùng Bill X" inside the `container` cell) — the defect is
the phone record renderer only. Reference shape that already works:
`ShipmentDebitTables.tsx` Bảng 2.1 marks its Cước thu column `primary: true`
(LedgerMatrix line 65) so it survives the phone collapse.

## Fix contract

The cước-thu money fact is promoted `primary` (static, same marking as
Bảng 2.1's Cước thu) on both boards' phone records: the 100%/0₫ lane and its
reason render on the card face, outside the 'Chi tiết' disclosure. Sibling
facts keep their current primary/secondary split (no other row contract
changes; FCL/ungrouped rows simply gain one visible money fact — the F-2
display law owns this column on every row).
