Compact, ordinary, phone and coarse-pointer controls share the same text size. No clipped text, fractional local scales or private 16px input exception. Preserve browser zoom and inspect physical-device focus behavior. |

All cases are scoped UI/interaction changes. Authorization, money calculations, data writes, approvals and offline behavior are unchanged. Browser result evidence belongs to the coordinating route scan; component checks alone do not establish all-device coverage.
