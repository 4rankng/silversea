# Direct actions without approval workflows

Customer decision: remove all application approval workflows now. Any future approval product needs separately agreed requirements; no dormant approval screen, maker-checker route or implicit approval gate belongs to the current flow.

This plan supersedes approval expectations in historical QA records. Historical evidence remains unchanged; a previous PASS does not verify these criteria. Factory-search regressions remain in `2026-09-17-factory-search.md`.

## Automated and manual coverage policy

- Any changed pure rule, label mapping, state reducer, component interaction or service branch receives a regression unit test. Money, permissions and legacy route retirement also require integration assertions; unit mocks alone do not prove persistence or isolation.
- Browser-only concerns (layout, clipped overlays, mobile scrolling, camera/file controls) retain the exact case above with role, viewport, action, expected result and evidence path. Do not call these PASS from source inspection or a unit test.
- Retain old approval records as audit evidence where necessary. Test that no new pending approval is created and no old approval route remains actionable. Do not delete financial history or auto-pay historical requests while removing a workflow.
- Test run status starts **NOT TESTED**. Record actual PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED and evidence in the task's QA report after execution; no historical completion label is accepted as current proof.
